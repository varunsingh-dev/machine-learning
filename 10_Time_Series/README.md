# ⏰ Time Series Analysis

## 📖 Overview

Time Series Analysis is used to analyze data collected over time. Unlike regular datasets, time series data contains a time-based order that helps us identify patterns, trends, and seasonal behavior.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this project is to:

- Understand time series data.
- Analyze data based on time.
- Identify trends and patterns.
- Visualize time-based data.
- Understand seasonality and forecasting concepts.

---

## 🧠 What is a Time Series?

A time series is a sequence of data points collected or recorded at different points in time.

Examples include:

- Daily sales
- Monthly revenue
- Stock prices
- Temperature records
- Website traffic
- Electricity consumption

---

## 📊 Components of Time Series

### 1. Trend

A long-term upward or downward movement in the data.

### 2. Seasonality

A repeating pattern that occurs at regular intervals.

### 3. Cyclic Pattern

A pattern that occurs over longer and irregular periods.

### 4. Random Variation

Unexpected changes or noise in the data.
